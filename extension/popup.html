How to download your chats
  1. Open chatgpt.com
  2. Click your profile picture (bottom-left corner)
  3. Click Settings → Data controls
  4. Click Export data → confirm
  5. Check your email — OpenAI sends a download link (may take a few minutes)
  6. Download the file — it will be a .zip file
